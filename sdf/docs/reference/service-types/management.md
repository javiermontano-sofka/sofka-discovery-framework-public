# Management — PMO, governance, portfolio

## Scope signal

- "Set up a PMO" · "Portfolio governance" · "Project methodology" · "Agile/Hybrid transformation" · "PMO maturity"

## Committee

Permanent triad + pmo-architect (lead, from pm:) · agile-coach · methodology-architect · governance specialists · change-catalyst.

## Pipeline variant

- P3 AS-IS = PMO maturity assessment (industry framework: P3M3 or similar).
- P5 scenarios = PMO models (supportive, controlling, directive) + methodology mix.
- G1.5 feasibility: organisational readiness for governance uplift.

## Typical deliverables beyond P0-P9

- PMO maturity score.
- Governance model + committee structure.
- Methodology playbook.
- Tooling roadmap (Jira, ADO, Monday, etc.).

## Anti-patterns

- "Manage our project" → operational delivery; out of SDF scope.
- "Train agile coaches" → Training program, Management-adjacent.
