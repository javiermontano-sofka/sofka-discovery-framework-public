Estrategia de Adopcion

Acme Corp — Modernizacion Core Bancario

Fecha: 13 de marzo de 2026 Modo: Estrategia (Full) Alcance: ~320 personas, 3 fases

TL;DR

  • Rollout en 3 fases: Piloto (S1-4) → Early Majority (S5-12) → Full (S13-20)
  • 24 mensajes planificados en calendario de comunicacion (6 canales, 5 grupos)
  • Curriculum: 47 horas, 80% micro-learning + hands-on, 20% classroom
  • Red de 15 champions (1:20 ratio), train-the-trainer en Semana 1-2
  • Target: 85% active use a Semana 20, 70% proficiency a Semana 24

Conclusiones

  1. 3-phase rollout minimizes risk: Back-Office adopts last when maximum evidence and support exist.
  2. Communication is 60% of the strategy: 24 planned messages, 6 channels, ADKAR-staged per group.
  3. Champion network is the multiplier: 15 champions reduce project team load and generate social proof.
  4. Training is micro-learning first: 80% modular, on-demand, just-in-time.
  5. Metrics with teeth: Every KPI has a threshold that triggers intervention.
